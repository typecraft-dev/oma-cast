## snapshot.json (the `rates` object)

Euro foreign exchange reference rates from the European Central Bank, by way of
[frankfurter.dev](https://frankfurter.dev), as of the date stored with them.

## symbols.woff2

A subset (only the icons the launcher draws) of JetBrainsMono Nerd Font from
[Nerd Fonts](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts), distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt` in
this directory). The icon sets Nerd Fonts collects keep their own licences, listed in
[its LICENSE file](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/blob/master/LICENSE).
